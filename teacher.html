Timing rules

  • Wrong answer: the room locks for 15 s, then restarts at question 1.
  • After 20 min (mercy rule): a wrong answer locks for 5 s and the team stays on the same question.
  • Wrong face in Lock 2: 10 s wait. Copy, paste or right-click: 10 s “Hold on” screen.
  • Locks never reset a room. Wrong codes and riddle guesses have no penalty.
  • “Pick two” questions need exactly the two ✓ options. Once two boxes are checked, the others grey out until one is unchecked.

What teams will ask you for

Lock 1: the Ghost’s code

Give the visiting teammate 412. You are the Ghost; a spooky voice is encouraged.

Lock 2: stuck on the audience

The guilty face (★) never moves. It is the only face that is pale, frowning and sweating.

★
  • Hint 1: “Look for the one face that isn’t smiling.”
  • Hint 2: “The King goes pale. Find the palest face.”
  • Hint 3: “Second row, fifth seat from the left.”

Lock 3: the riddle

Answer: revenge (vengeance also works). Any typed answer containing the word counts (“revenge!”, “getting revenge”).

  • Clue 1: “What did Hamlet promise the Ghost in Act 1?”
  • Clue 2: “Laertes wants it for the same reason Hamlet does.”
  • Clue 3: “It starts with R.”

General clue for any question

Every answer comes from the act named on the room. Point teams to the “Check it” note in the key below.

Answer key

Options appear here in data order. On screen they are shuffled. ✓ = correct, ✗ = wrong. Each room has one joke option, always wrong.

Room 1 The Castle Wall

Act 1: A ghost with a message

Q1 · Skill: Mood

The play opens at midnight with nervous guards on a cold, dark castle wall. What mood does this opening create?

  • ✓Tense and uneasy, like something is wrong in Denmark.
  • ✗Cheerful and festive, like a royal celebration.
  • ✗Calm and peaceful, like a quiet night at home.
  • ✗Silly and lighthearted, like the start of a comedy.

Check it: 1.1: It is just past midnight, bitterly cold, and the guards are jumpy because they have seen the Ghost twice before.

Q2 · Act 1

Even before he sees the Ghost, why is Hamlet so upset?

  • ✓His father died, and his mother married his uncle about a month later.
  • ✗Claudius has banished him from Denmark for good.
  • ✗He lost a sword fight to Laertes in front of the whole court.
  • ✗Horatio told the King all of Hamlet’s secrets.

Check it: 1.2, Hamlet’s first soliloquy: his mother remarried “within a month” of his father’s funeral, to his father’s brother.

Q3 · Act 1 · pick two

What does the Ghost tell Hamlet?

  • ✓Claudius killed him by pouring poison in his ear while he slept.
  • ✓Hamlet should get revenge, but leave his mother’s punishment to heaven.
  • ✗Gertrude helped Claudius plan the murder.
  • ✗Hamlet should save him a plate of the wedding leftovers.

Check it: 1.5: the Ghost says Claudius poured poison in his ear while he slept in the orchard, and tells Hamlet to leave his mother to heaven. He never says Gertrude helped plan the murder.

Room 2 The Hallway

Act 2: Everyone is watching Hamlet

Q1 · Skill: Character

An actor cries real tears while performing a sad speech about a queen from an old story. Why does this make Hamlet angry at himself?

  • ✓The actor shows more emotion over a story than Hamlet has shown about his own father’s murder.
  • ✗Hamlet thinks the actor is secretly making fun of his mother.
  • ✗Hamlet wanted to perform the speech himself and was not asked.
  • ✗The speech reminds Hamlet that Ophelia turned him down.

Check it: End of 2.2, Hamlet’s soliloquy after the actor’s Hecuba speech: the actor weeps over a made-up story while Hamlet has done nothing about a real murder.

Q2 · Act 2

Polonius tells the King and Queen he has figured out why Hamlet is acting strange. What is his theory?

  • ✓Hamlet has gone mad because Ophelia rejected his love.
  • ✗Hamlet caught a fever on the trip home from school.
  • ✗Hamlet is upset that Polonius gave him bad advice.
  • ✗Hamlet is secretly planning to run away with the actors.

Check it: 2.1–2.2: Polonius believes Hamlet went mad after Ophelia, on her father’s orders, refused his letters and his visits.

Q3 · Act 2 · pick two

Which of these happen in Act 2?

  • ✓Rosencrantz and Guildenstern admit that the King and Queen sent for them.
  • ✓Hamlet asks the actors to perform a murder play, with some lines he will add.
  • ✗Hamlet stabs Polonius through a curtain.
  • ✗The actors cancel the show because they want to go home for Thanksgiving.

Check it: 2.2: Guildenstern admits “we were sent for,” and Hamlet asks the actors for The Murder of Gonzago with a short speech he will write. Polonius is stabbed in 3.4.

Room 3 The Queen’s Room

Act 3: The trap is sprung

Q1 · Skill: Dramatic irony

Hamlet finds Claudius kneeling to pray and decides not to kill him yet. What does the audience know that Hamlet doesn’t?

  • ✓Claudius can’t really pray. He knows his words aren’t reaching heaven.
  • ✗Polonius is hiding nearby, ready to warn the King.
  • ✗Claudius has already confessed everything to Gertrude.
  • ✗The Ghost has changed its mind about revenge.

Check it: 3.3: before Hamlet enters, Claudius says he cannot pray; after Hamlet leaves, he admits his words fly up but his thoughts stay below.

Q2 · Act 3

Why does Hamlet kill Polonius?

  • ✓He hears someone hiding behind the curtain in his mother’s room and thinks it might be the King.
  • ✗Polonius tells the King about Hamlet’s plan to use the actors.
  • ✗Polonius attacks Hamlet with a sword first.
  • ✗The Ghost tells Hamlet to do it.

Check it: 3.4: Hamlet hears a voice behind the curtain, stabs through it, and asks, “Is it the King?”

Q3 · Act 3 · pick two

Which of these happen in Act 3?

  • ✓Hamlet harshly rejects Ophelia while her father and the King secretly watch.
  • ✓The Ghost appears in Gertrude’s room, but only Hamlet can see it.
  • ✗Laertes breaks into the castle with an angry crowd behind him.
  • ✗Claudius stops the play to ask for a second slice of pie.

Check it: 3.1 is the scene with Ophelia while Polonius and Claudius hide; in 3.4 the Ghost returns and Gertrude sees nothing. Laertes and the mob arrive in 4.5.

Room 4 The Throne Room

Act 4: Grief and revenge

Q1 · Skill: Foil

Laertes and Hamlet have both lost a father. How does Laertes act as a foil to Hamlet in Act 4?

  • ✓Laertes rushes into action right away, while Hamlet has put off revenge for months.
  • ✗Laertes forgives his father’s killer, while Hamlet holds a grudge.
  • ✗Laertes refuses to listen to Claudius, while Hamlet trusts him completely.
  • ✗Laertes cares less about his family’s honor than Hamlet does.

Check it: 4.5 and 4.7: Laertes storms the castle the moment he hears; Hamlet calls his own revenge “dull” in 4.4.

Q2 · Act 4

Claudius sends a sealed letter to England along with Hamlet. What does it say?

  • ✓Put Hamlet to death as soon as he arrives.
  • ✗Crown Hamlet as a prince of England.
  • ✗Send soldiers to help Denmark fight Norway.
  • ✗Keep Hamlet safe until the Queen calms down.

Check it: End of 4.3: Claudius’s letter orders England to carry out “the present death of Hamlet.”

Q3 · Act 4 · pick two

Which of these happen in Act 4?

  • ✓Pirates attack Hamlet’s ship, and he ends up back in Denmark.
  • ✓Ophelia hands out flowers and sings sad songs.
  • ✗Hamlet and Laertes finally fight their fencing match.
  • ✗Horatio hosts Friendsgiving, and Claudius brings the drinks.

Check it: 4.6: Hamlet’s letter to Horatio tells of the pirates; 4.5: Ophelia’s flowers and songs. The fencing match is only planned in 4.7; it happens in Act 5.

The madness vote

Shown after Room 1, before Lock 1. No wrong answer, and the team’s choice comes back on the final screen. Use it as the main-room debrief.

“An act”

  • At the end of Act 1, Hamlet tells Horatio and Marcellus he may pretend to be strange.
  • He tells Rosencrantz and Guildenstern he is only mad some of the time (2.2), and Polonius notices there is a method to it.
  • In 3.4 he tells his mother he is not really mad, only pretending.

“Really losing it”

  • He stabs through a curtain without knowing who is there (3.4).
  • He sees and speaks to the Ghost while Gertrude sees nothing (3.4).
  • His treatment of Ophelia in 3.1 swings wildly, and he says himself he has lost all his joy (2.2).

Surprises (none affect scoring)

  • Hidden turkey: bottom-right corner of the Room 1 picture panel (also during the madness vote). Clicking it adds a “Turkey found” badge to the final screen.
  • The curtain: curtain icon in the header. It fades the question for 3 seconds with a Polonius joke and helps no one.
  • Leftovers clock: a nod to Hamlet’s joke in 1.2 that the funeral food was served cold at the wedding. Changes every 3 min: Still warm → Lukewarm → Room temp → Wedding buffet → Getting cold → Cold → Very cold → Science project. The final screen reads “You escaped while the leftovers were …”.
  • Lockout lines: “The Ghost is disappointed. Again.” · “Polonius has a theory about that answer. Please don’t ask him.” · “Rosencrantz and Guildenstern are reporting this to the King.” · “Something moved behind the curtain. Wait.” · “The leftovers are getting colder.” · “Horatio is writing this down.”
  • One joke option per room (always wrong): the wedding leftovers, actors going home for Thanksgiving, Claudius wanting more pie, and Horatio’s Friendsgiving.
